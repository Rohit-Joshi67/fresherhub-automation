About FresherHub

FresherHub was founded to bridge the information gap for fresh engineering graduates, BCA/MCA students, and early-career developers entering the tech and public sectors in India.

Zero Fees, Zero Scams: FresherHub is 100% free. We never charge candidates for job listings, mock test materials, or placement assistance. Every application link points directly to the employer's official portal.

What We Do

  • Official ATS Tracking: We index verified entry-level job openings directly from enterprise Applicant Tracking Systems (such as Greenhouse, Lever, SmartRecruiters, and Workday) and official company career pages.
  • Structured Preparation Roadmaps: Rather than merely listing job roles, we curate technical question banks (Data Structures, SQL, Java/Spring Boot, System Basics) and Government exam syllabus breakdowns (SSC CGL, RRB NTPC, IBPS SO, NIC Scientist-B).
  • No Ghost Jobs or Sponsored Junk: Every indexed opportunity is checked for entry-level suitability, graduation batch eligibility (2024–2026 batches), and authentic domain ownership.
