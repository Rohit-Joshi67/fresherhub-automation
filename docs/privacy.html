Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 2026

At FresherHub (freshersjobopening.online), accessible from https://freshersjobopening.online, candidate privacy and transparency are paramount. This Privacy Policy documents what information is received, how it is used, and your rights.

1. Cookies and Third-Party Advertising (Google AdSense)

FresherHub utilizes third-party advertising partners, including Google AdSense, to support free access to our job aggregation platform and preparation resources.

  • Google uses cookies, including the DoubleClick DART cookie, to serve relevant advertisements to visitors based on prior visits to our site and other destinations across the web.
  • Users can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings at adssettings.google.com.
  • You may also opt out of third-party vendor cookies for personalized ads via aboutads.info.

2. No Collection of Candidate Application Data

FresherHub never prompts candidates for resumes, national IDs, or fee payments. All application links route directly to third-party employer Applicant Tracking Systems (e.g., Lever, Greenhouse, Workday, SmartRecruiters). Candidate data submitted on those portals is governed strictly by the respective employer's privacy policy.

3. Log Files & Analytics

Like standard web hosts, FresherHub logs basic non-personally identifiable visit statistics (including IP addresses, browser user-agents, referral pages, and timestamps) solely for platform stability, security auditing, and server performance monitoring.

4. Consent & Contact

By using our website, you hereby consent to our Privacy Policy and agree to its terms. If you have any inquiries regarding our privacy standards, contact our Data & Compliance team at:
contact@freshersjobopening.online
