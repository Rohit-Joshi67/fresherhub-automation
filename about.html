About FresherHub

FresherHub was founded to bridge the information gap for fresh graduates and early-career developers entering the tech industry in India.

What We Do

  • Official Portal Tracking: We index job openings directly from enterprise applicant tracking systems (ATS), cutting out unverified third-party job listings.
  • Prep Roadmaps: Rather than just pasting job specs, every opening is paired with topic breakdowns (DSA, frameworks, SQL, and system expectations) to help candidates prepare.

Editorial & Aggregation Disclaimer

FresherHub is an independent aggregator and technical educational portal. All company names, brand assets, and trademark references belong exclusively to their respective corporate entities. We never charge job seekers any recruitment fees.
